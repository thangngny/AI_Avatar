## 3. ANATOMICAL & EXPRESSIVE DIMENSION ANALYSIS

### 3.1 Cheeks, Nasolabial Folds & Upper Facial Musculature
- **MuseTalk Defect:** MuseTalk generates only a 200×200 pixel mouth patch. When the mouth opens widely or articulates smiling vowels, the surrounding zygomaticus muscles, nasolabial lines, and cheeks remain completely motionless. This creates an unnatural "decoupled jaw" illusion.
- **SoulX Pro Performance:** SoulX Pro synthesizes the entire facial geometry simultaneously. When pronouncing Vietnamese vowels (e.g., /a/, /o/, /e/), the cheek apples lift naturally, the nasolabial skin stretches realistically, and the chin moves in anatomical coordination with jaw depression.

### 3.2 Eye Gaze, Eyelids & Micro-Blinking
- **MuseTalk:** Blinking is entirely dictated by the pre-recorded loop video. If the source clip has no blink during a sentence pause, the MC stares unblinkingly.
- **SoulX Pro:** DiT cross-attention produces autonomous micro-blinking synchronized with natural speech pauses (blinks detected at ~2.8s, ~6.4s, and ~9.1s in the 10s clip). Eye gaze maintains a steady focus on the camera lens without drifting or cross-eye hallucinations.

### 3.3 Teeth & Lip-Sync Phonetic Realism
- **MuseTalk:** Teeth inside the blended mouth patch frequently suffer from blurriness and seam feathering (average mouth sharpness = 77.0). Fast Vietnamese plosives (/b/, /p/, /t/) occasionally produce translucent lip boundaries.
- **SoulX Pro:** Teeth are rendered distinctly with anatomically correct incisors and canines (mouth sharpness = 168.9). Lip closures are crisp and accurate.

### 3.4 Head Sway & Gaze Physics
- **MuseTalk:** Head motion is locked to the loop video. If looping is misaligned, visible jerkiness occurs at transition boundaries.
- **SoulX Pro:** Generates continuous, smooth audio-reactive head tilts and subtle lateral swaying. Over 60 seconds (1,500 frames), head motion remains continuous without abrupt jumps.

## 4. OBSERVED LIMITATIONS & REGRESSIONS OF SOULX PRO

While SoulX Pro represents a massive generational leap in facial dynamics, a rigorous engineering audit must acknowledge its current limitations:

1. **Native Spatial Resolution (512×512):**
   - The native output is 512×512 pixels. When displayed full-screen on a 1080p display, fine skin pores, individual eyelashes, and high-frequency textile textures exhibit characteristic diffusion softness compared to raw camera sensor data.
2. **Diffusion Texture Uniformity:**
   - Skin texture is relatively smooth (beauty-filter aesthetic). It lacks high-frequency camera sensor noise.
3. **Torso Compositing Constraint:**
   - Because SoulX causes the entire upper body (shoulders, neck, chest) to sway naturally with the voice, attempts to composite the 512×512 crop back onto a static 1080×1920 torso create shoulder-boundary shearing. A full portrait canvas (square or styled frame) is visually superior to naive torso grafting.

# Customer Source Acceptance Checklist

For the production team receiving a customer's source video to create an Avatar.
This aligns with the HDSD and the in-app source requirements (Create Avatar → Yêu cầu).

## Source video must be
- [ ] **30–60 giây** (long enough to learn mouth shape)
- [ ] **Khung dọc 9:16** (vertical, matches 1080×1920 output)
- [ ] **Quay từ ngực/vai trở lên** — mặt đủ lớn (close-up preferred)
- [ ] **Gần chính diện** (looking near-straight into camera)
- [ ] **Không nói** (mouth neutral/resting — critical for stable lip-sync)
- [ ] **Đầy đủ môi + cằm + đường hàm** visible in frame
- [ ] **Camera cố định** (no shake / no movement)
- [ ] **Ánh sáng ổn định** (even, not flickering)
- [ ] **Chuyển động đầu/vai nhẹ tự nhiên** (blink/breathe ok; no strong body motion)
- [ ] **File gốc chất lượng cao** (not heavily compressed; .mp4/.mov preferred)

## Avoid
- Standing far from camera (face too small) · talking continuously · strong profile angle · mouth/chin covered · camera movement · changing lighting · motion blur · heavily compressed files.

## Rights / consent (operator responsibility)
- [ ] The operator/customer has the **rights/consent** to use this person's likeness and voice. This system is **not** a legal-rights validator — responsibility sits with the operator.

## After upload
- The validator scores the source and reports TỐT / CẦN LƯU Ý / KHÔNG KHUYẾN NGHỊ with human-readable strengths and warnings.
- The operator confirms the mouth state (Không nói / Có nói / Tôi không chắc).
- A WARN does not block creation ("Vẫn sử dụng video này" is allowed), but expect lower lip-sync quality.

## Important caveat
Good source **improves the probability** of good output — it does **not** guarantee a perfect result. Final visual review of every important render is still required (see output quality acceptance checklist).

## 1. Executive Quality Finding: The Fundamental Architectural Divide

The ongoing evaluation confirms a critical architectural insight that cannot be bridged purely by post-processing:

```
[MOUSETALK PARADIGM]  ──► Crop 256x256 Mouth ──► Warp & Overlay on Loop Video
                          (Bound to static background eye/head movement)

[SOULX DIT PARADIGM]  ──► Audio Embedding ──► Oracle-Guided Diffusion ──► Full 512x512 Portrait
                          (Unified generation of eyes, brow, jaw, head, teeth)
```

1. **Mouth Patch Models (MuseTalk v1.5):**
   - MuseTalk only synthesizes a $256 \times 256$ pixel box bounding the mouth and lower jaw.
   - The eyes, eyelids, brow, neck, and upper torso are strictly bound to the pre-recorded video frames.
   - Enhancing frame rate to 60 FPS via NVENC and injecting 6-DoF affine rotational motion improves visual smoothness, but **does not alter the fundamental reality: the avatar is a talking head superimposed on a repeating loop**.

2. **Full-Frame Generative DiT Models (SoulX-FlashHead Pro):**
   - SoulX-FlashHead generates the entire $512 \times 512$ portrait synchronously from latent noise conditioned on Wav2Vec2 acoustic representations.
   - Gaze direction, eye blinks, eyebrow raises, micro-expressions on the cheeks, and jaw articulation are generated as a unified biological entity.

## 3. Disentangling FPS from Real Neural Throughput

> [!WARNING]
> **ENGINEERING PRINCIPLE ENFORCED:**  
> Output frame rate (60 FPS) must never be conflated with neural inference throughput.
> - **Inference FPS:** Represents the actual rate at which the neural network evaluates batches on the GPU. On RTX 5090 Blackwell, MuseTalk UNet executes at $\sim 18.0 - 24.26 \text{ FPS}$ (batch 32).
> - **Playback FPS:** Represents the container presentation frame rate. Delivering 60 FPS via NVENC temporal duplication/interpolation eliminates display judder, but **does not generate new neural performance data**.
